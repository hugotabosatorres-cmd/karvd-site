2. Our lawful bases

  • Contract (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)): your account, plan, training, nutrition, social features and purchases.
  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a), and Article 9(2)(a) for health data): Apple Health, body photos and the body-fat estimate, GPS routes, sending photos and text to AI services, the safety checks on what you publish, coach calls and check-ins, the form check, importing your training from screenshots, the period check-in, the weekly Shop pick, analytics and contacts. You can withdraw these as described in section 8. Withdrawing does not undo what we did before.
  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)): keeping the community safe (reports, and limits on how often something can be done), fixing faults, and install attribution: working out which creator’s link led to an install and a Premium purchase, so the creator can be paid. We have weighed these interests against your rights and expectations, and you can object (see “Your rights”).
  • Legal obligation: tax and accounting records for sales.

Install attribution. It is not advertising and it does not track you. karvd uses a version of the attribution software without advertising-identifier code, does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) or the vendor identifier (IDFV), does not use Apple Search Ads or SKAdNetwork attribution, does not connect it to any advertising network, and never shows Apple’s tracking prompt. karvd sends it no events of its own: the app never tells it what you do in karvd, and it never receives health, training, body or social data. We rely on our legitimate interest, not your consent, for this one purpose. You can object by emailing support@karvd.app (see section 8).

3. Apple Health and health data

If you connect Apple Health, karvd asks to read your workouts (with their distances), sleep, daily step count, and the heart rate recorded during workouts. It asks to write the sessions you record in karvd as workouts, and their route only if you turn that on. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings › Health.

  • We use Health data only to run the app’s features for you: counting sessions towards your plan, recovery, FitScore and insights.
  • We never use Health data for advertising, marketing or data mining, and we never share or sell it. It is not sent to analytics, to attribution, to the Shop, or to AI services.
  • Heart rate is kept as an average and a maximum for each session, not as a stream.
  • On Apple Watch, karvd reads your heart rate during a workout you start in karvd, to show it on the watch and save the session’s average and maximum. It does not read your heart rate at any other time.
  • Daily step counts are read from Apple Health on your phone to work out your FitScore and are not saved on our servers. The other Health data we hold (workouts and sleep) is deleted when you delete your account (see section 8).
  • karvd keeps its local database out of iCloud Backup.

karvd is not a medical device. It gives general fitness guidance, not medical advice. Please check with your doctor before you start or change your training, eating or medication, and if anything hurts or feels wrong. The AI coach says the same.

5. AI services, in plain words

Some features send content to AI services. Each has its own consent or notice, shown before the first use:

  • Meals and equipment: photos or text you choose, to recognise food and gym equipment. “Estimate with AI” in the food search sends the name of the food you typed (and a few similar foods’ nutrition values).
  • Goal Wall: your cropped body photos, to estimate a body-fat range and draw illustrations. Estimates are approximate, not medical measurements.
  • Insights: a summary of your training as numbers, to write a short text.
  • Form check (Premium): the measurements your phone takes from a video of your set, as numbers, to write feedback in your coach’s voice. The video never leaves your phone. It is technique coaching, not medical advice.
  • Import your training from another app: the first time, karvd asks you to agree. The screenshots you pick (and only those) are sent to an AI service, which reads the exercises, sets, reps and weights in them. They are deleted from your phone once read, and karvd does not store them or write what is in them in any log. You see what was read and check it before anything is saved, and karvd’s own rules (not AI) suggest changes you can take or leave. The AI service keeps what it was sent for the period in the table in section 4 (text AI). It is not medical advice. You can withdraw in Settings › Privacy › “Import your training”; the server then refuses to read screenshots, and what you already saved as routines or a plan stays yours.
  • Safety checks on what you publish: before your first post, comment, profile photo, group check-in photo or name that others will see, karvd asks you to agree. It then checks, with AI, the words you write (sent to an AI text service) and the photos and videos you share (sent to an AI image service) before other people see them. If the check is unsure about a photo, a person at karvd looks at it before anyone else does. If you do not agree you can still browse, react, follow people and use the rest of karvd, but you cannot publish anything others would see. You can change your mind in Settings › Privacy › “Safety checks on what I post”: switching it off stops you publishing and stops new content being sent; what you already posted stays, and anything still waiting for its check stays hidden and is not sent until you agree again.
  • Coach calls and written check-ins: see section 6.

You can tell us an AI answer is wrong with “Report this answer” under it (section 1).

AI output can be wrong. A person can always override an estimate.

What our AI providers commit to. On the plans we use, our text AI and image AI providers do not use what we send them to train their models, and keep it only for a limited time (see “What our service providers keep” in section 4). The speech-to-text service is told to opt out of using your audio to improve its models. The voice generation service receives only the coach’s words as text, never your voice; its terms are set out in the table.

6. Coach calls (Premium)

Premium includes live voice calls, and written check-ins, with an AI coach. They are optional and need your coach call consent, which you can withdraw in Settings. The coach is an AI, not a person. It is not a medical professional: it gives fitness guidance, not medical advice, and it will tell you to check with your doctor.

  • The audio is streamed live and is not recorded. No recordings or transcripts are stored by the call services. Recording and transcript storage are switched off at the live call service.
  • Who handles the call while it runs: our live call service (connects the call, US), the service that runs the AI coach (London, UK), a speech-to-text service (US), a text-to-speech service for the coach’s voice (US) and an AI text service (writes the coach’s replies, US). See section 4.
  • What karvd keeps afterwards: short notes written after each call (a summary, where you stopped, open items, any commitment made, any plan change you accepted, and which coach led the call), the insight cards prepared for your check-in from your own training data, and call times (when the call happened, how long it lasted and how it ended). The words of the call are held in memory while it runs and are discarded once the notes are written. The notes are one shared memory, so that any coach can pick up where the last one left off.
  • Control: the notes are part of your data export and are deleted with your account. If you withdraw the coach call consent, the notes are emptied.
  • Safety: the coach does not give medical advice. For anything medical it says it cannot give medical advice and that you should check with a doctor. If you tell it about something urgent happening now, such as chest pain, dizziness or trouble breathing, it says “I’m not capable of giving medical advice. Please consult with a doctor.”, asks you to stop exercising, and asks whether you want to continue the call or stop. See the Terms.

7. Your contacts

Only if you tap Connect contacts in Search (Contacts tab), and allow it in iOS, karvd uses your address book to show you which of your contacts are already on karvd.

  • What is read: the email addresses (and names, to label the list on your screen). karvd does not read phone numbers, street addresses, birthdays, photos or notes.
  • How it is checked: on your phone, each email address is trimmed, put in lower case and turned into a scrambled code (a SHA-256 hash). Only those codes are sent to karvd, in batches. Your contacts’ names and the email addresses themselves never leave your phone. Because a scrambled email is still derived from an email address, we treat the codes as personal data.
  • What karvd keeps: nothing. The codes are compared with the codes of karvd members and then discarded: they are not saved and not written to logs. karvd keeps only a count of how many times you checked today, to stop people guessing email addresses.
  • Who can be found: only people who turned on “Let friends find me” and whose email is confirmed. Anyone you blocked, who blocked you, or you muted is left out. If someone has your email in their contacts and you turned “Let friends find me” on, they can find you; they only see your name and picture.
  • Staying in control: tap Disconnect contacts on the Contacts tab to make karvd forget that you connected (that choice is stored on your phone only). You can also switch Contacts off for karvd in iOS Settings, or turn off “Let friends find me” to stop others finding you this way. Disconnecting does not need an account request; there is nothing on karvd’s servers to delete.
  • Inviting: the Invite button opens your phone’s share sheet with a private invite link. karvd never sends a message itself.

Deleting your data. In Settings › Account and data › Delete account and data, you can ask us to delete your account. We delete your files first, then your account and everything linked to it, and keep a deletion receipt with no health data. You can follow the request under Deletion request status. Deleting the app alone does not delete your account.

When you delete your account, karvd also asks our subscription management, usage analytics and install attribution services to delete what they hold about you. They do it on their own timetable, usually within 30 days. We ask only once your account is fully deleted, and a supplier that cannot be reached is asked again later; it never holds up your deletion. For install attribution, karvd asks for every install id our server has listed for your account (from every phone you used, once that phone had run this version of the app), together with the id of the phone you delete from and any id saved with an invite before 15 November 2026. Each id is removed from our list once the service confirms the erasure. Two limits: for usage analytics, karvd can only send the id held by the phone you delete from, so usage events tied to an earlier analytics id (from before you withdrew the analytics consent, which gives the phone a new id, or from another phone) cannot be found and are not deleted; they carry no name, email or account id. And an install id from a phone that has not opened this version of the app, or from before it, is not on our list and cannot be erased by us. “Delete account and data” also removes the creator labels, and your period check-ins, from your phone.

9. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to: tell you what we hold, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, give you a portable copy, and stop automated decisions that affect you (karvd makes none with legal effect). Use the in-app tools above, or write to support@karvd.app. You can object to install attribution at any time by email (section 8). If you ask us by email for what we hold about you, we will also obtain what our service providers (section 4) hold about you and send it with our reply. We reply within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

10. Children

karvd is for people aged 18 and over. It asks for your date of birth and does not let younger people in. If you think a child has an account, write to us and we will delete it.

11. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Files are in private storage with short-lived links. If you use a karvd password, our sign-in system stores it only as a salted hash, and it must be at least 8 characters; Apple and Google sign-in use no karvd password at all. Server keys for AI services are kept on the server only. karvd keeps its local database out of iCloud Backup. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach puts your rights at risk we will tell you and the ICO as the law requires.

12. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it applies. We keep previous versions and will send one on request to support@karvd.app.

Safety screens

karvd remembers the date you saw its safety information, on your phone and with your account, so it does not show it again on a new phone. It does not ask for or store any health details for this.
