2. Our lawful bases

  • Contract (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)): your account, plan, training, nutrition, social features and purchases.
  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a), and Article 9(2)(a) for health data): Apple Health, body photos and the body-fat estimate, injuries, GPS routes, sending photos and text to AI services, coach calls, analytics, contacts. You can withdraw any of these in Settings. Withdrawing does not undo what we did before.
  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)): keeping the community safe (checking posts), preventing abuse (including repeat invite redemptions from one device), fixing faults, and working out which invite link brought a friend to karvd (install attribution). We have weighed these interests against your rights and expectations, and you can object (see “Your rights”).
  • Legal obligation: tax and accounting records for purchases.

Install attribution and the rules on storing information on your phone (UK PECR). To tell which invite link brought you in, the invite-attribution software in karvd stores a random install id on your phone and reads it. We do not ask you first. We rely on the exemption for storing and accessing information for statistical purposes that the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 added to the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations. That exemption needs two things, and this is how we give them: clear information (this policy, and the text under the switch in Settings) and a simple way to object (Settings › Privacy › “Invite links: let karvd see which invite brought you in”, which is on until you turn it off). When you turn it off, the software stops at once and does not start again at any later launch. This is separate from the optional analytics choice, which stays off until you agree. It is not advertising: karvd does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) or the vendor identifier (IDFV), does not use Apple Search Ads or SKAdNetwork attribution, does not connect it to advertising networks, and never shows Apple’s tracking prompt.

3. Apple Health and health data

If you connect Apple Health, karvd asks to read your workouts (with their distances), sleep, daily step count, and the heart rate recorded during workouts. It asks to write the sessions you record in karvd as workouts, and their route only if you turn that on. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings › Health.

  • We use Health data only to run the app’s features for you: counting sessions towards your plan, recovery, FitScore and insights.
  • We never use Health data for advertising, marketing or data mining, and we never share or sell it. It is not sent to analytics, to attribution, to the Shop, or to AI services.
  • Heart rate is kept as an average and a maximum for each session, not as a stream.
  • Daily step counts are read from Apple Health on your phone to work out your FitScore and are not saved on our servers. The other Health data we hold (workouts and sleep) is deleted when you delete your account (see section 8).
  • karvd keeps its local database out of iCloud Backup.

karvd is not a medical device. It gives general fitness guidance, not medical advice.

5. AI services, in plain words

Some features send content to AI services. Each has its own consent or notice, shown before the first use:

  • Meals and equipment: photos or text you choose, to recognise food and gym equipment.
  • Goal Wall: your cropped body photos, to estimate a body-fat range and draw illustrations. Estimates are approximate, not medical measurements.
  • Insights: a summary of your training as numbers, to write a short text.
  • Safety checks on social posts: text, photos and videos in posts and comments, and display, circle and challenge names, are checked automatically for safety by AI services before they appear. You see a notice before your first post or comment.
  • Coach calls: see section 6.

AI output can be wrong. A person can always override an estimate.

What our AI providers commit to. Our AI providers’ terms do not allow them to use your data to train or improve their models. They keep it only for a limited time for abuse and safety monitoring, unless the law requires longer.

6. Coach calls (Premium)

Premium includes live voice calls with an AI coach. Calls are optional and need your coach call consent, which you can withdraw in Settings. The coach is an AI, not a person.

  • The audio is streamed live and is not recorded. No recordings or transcripts are stored by the call services. Recording and transcript storage are switched off at the live call service.
  • Who handles the call while it runs: our live call service (connects the call, US), the service that runs the AI coach (London, UK), a speech-to-text service (US), a text-to-speech service for the coach’s voice (US) and an AI text service (writes the coach’s replies, US). See section 4.
  • What karvd keeps afterwards: short notes written after each call (a summary, where you stopped, open items, any commitment made, any plan change you accepted, and which coach led the call), the insight cards prepared for your check-in from your own training data, and call times (when the call happened, how long it lasted and how it ended). The words of the call are held in memory while it runs and are discarded once the notes are written. The notes are one shared memory, so that any coach can pick up where the last one left off.
  • Control: the notes are part of your data export and are deleted with your account. If you withdraw the coach call consent, the notes are emptied.
  • Safety: the coach does not give medical advice. See the Terms.

7. Your contacts

Only if you tap Connect contacts in Search (Contacts tab), and allow it in iOS, karvd uses your address book to show you which of your contacts are already on karvd.

  • What is read: the email addresses (and names, to label the list on your screen). karvd does not read phone numbers, street addresses, birthdays, photos or notes.
  • How it is checked: on your phone, each email address is trimmed, put in lower case and turned into a scrambled code (a SHA-256 hash). Only those codes are sent to karvd, in batches. Your contacts’ names and the email addresses themselves never leave your phone. Because a scrambled email is still derived from an email address, we treat the codes as personal data.
  • What karvd keeps: nothing. The codes are compared with the codes of karvd members and then discarded: they are not saved and not written to logs. karvd keeps only a count of how many times you checked today, to stop people guessing email addresses.
  • Who can be found: only people who turned on “Let friends find me” and whose email is confirmed. Anyone you blocked, who blocked you, or you muted is left out. If someone has your email in their contacts and you turned “Let friends find me” on, they can find you; they only see your name and picture.
  • Staying in control: tap Disconnect contacts on the Contacts tab to make karvd forget that you connected (that choice is stored on your phone only). You can also switch Contacts off for karvd in iOS Settings, or turn off “Let friends find me” to stop others finding you this way. Disconnecting does not need an account request; there is nothing on karvd’s servers to delete.
  • Inviting: the Invite button opens your phone’s share sheet with a private invite link. karvd never sends a message itself.

Deleting your data. In Settings › Account and data › Delete account and data, you can ask us to delete your account. We delete your files first, then your account and everything linked to it, and keep a deletion receipt with no health data. You can follow the request under Deletion request status. Deleting the app alone does not delete your account.

When you delete your account, karvd also asks our subscription management, usage analytics and invite-attribution services to delete what they hold about you. They do it on their own timetable, usually within 30 days. We ask only once your account is fully deleted, and a supplier that cannot be reached is asked again later; it never holds up your deletion. Two limits: karvd can only send the analytics and install ids held by the phone you delete from. So usage events tied to an earlier analytics id (from before you withdrew the analytics consent, which gives the phone a new id, or from another phone) cannot be found and are not deleted; they carry no name, email or account id. And with “Invite links” switched off, the phone does not send its install id; the install id kept with an invite record is still erased.

Withdrawing a consent. Each consent (Apple Health, GPS, Goal Wall, AI, coach calls, analytics, contacts) can be withdrawn in Settings, or in iOS Settings. What you withdraw stops being used from that point. Install attribution is not a consent: it runs under the exemption described in section 2, and you object to it with the switch “Invite links: let karvd see which invite brought you in” in Settings › Privacy. Turning it off stops it at once and at every later launch. It does not recall what the invite-attribution service already received, and it does not delete the install id already kept with an invite record; deleting your account does. Invite links still open the invite screen with the switch off.

Export. In Settings › Account and data › Export my data you can get a copy of your data.

9. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to: tell you what we hold, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, give you a portable copy, and stop automated decisions that affect you (karvd makes none with legal effect). Use the in-app tools above, or write to support@karvd.app. You can object to install attribution at any time with the switch in Settings (section 2). We reply within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

10. Children

karvd is for people aged 18 and over. It asks for your date of birth and does not let younger people in. If you think a child has an account, write to us and we will delete it.

11. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Files are in private storage with short-lived links. If you use a karvd password, our sign-in system stores it only as a salted hash, and it must be at least 8 characters; Apple and Google sign-in use no karvd password at all. Server keys for AI services are kept on the server only. karvd keeps its local database out of iCloud Backup. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach puts your rights at risk we will tell you and the ICO as the law requires.

12. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it applies. We keep previous versions and will send one on request to support@karvd.app.

Safety screens

karvd remembers the date you saw its safety information, on your phone and with your account, so it does not show it again on a new phone. It does not ask for or store any health details for this. The same is true of the date you saw the notice about safety checks on posts.
