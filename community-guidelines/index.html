karvd community guidelines

karvd is a place to train, share progress and encourage each other. These guidelines apply to everything you post or send in karvd: posts, photos, videos, comments, reactions, names, circles, challenges and nudges. They sit alongside our Terms of Service.

Be kind

  • Encourage people. Do not mock, insult, threaten or harass anyone, including about their body, weight, shape, age, ability or how they train.
  • No hate. Do not attack people for their race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability or age.

Keep bodies and health safe

  • Progress photos are welcome. Nudity and sexual content are not. Keep underwear and swimwear photos to what you would show at a pool or gym.
  • Do not encourage starving, purging, extreme dieting, dangerous weight loss or self-harm. Do not post “pro-ana” or similar content.
  • Do not sell, promote or give doses for steroids, prescription medicines (including weight-loss injections) or other drugs.
  • Do not give medical advice or claim that something treats or cures a condition.

Adults only

  • karvd is for people aged 18 and over. Do not post photos or videos of children.

Be honest and respect others

  • Do not pretend to be someone else, post spam or advertise.
  • Only post photos, videos and music you have the right to share. Do not post other people without their permission.
  • Do not share anyone’s private information, such as where they live, their phone number or their location.

Nothing illegal

  • Do not post anything that is illegal in the UK, including terrorist content, child sexual abuse material, fraud and threats of violence.

What happens if something breaks these guidelines

  • Posts and comments are checked, by automated tools and by people, before or after others can see them. Something that breaks these guidelines is not published, or is removed.
  • We may also limit or suspend an account, or close it for serious or repeated breaches.
  • You can report a post, comment or person from its menu, and you can block or mute anyone.
  • If you think we got a decision wrong, email support@karvd.app and we will look at it again.

We may update these guidelines. We will tell you in the app when we make an important change.
